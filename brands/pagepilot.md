# PagePilot · brand profile

Real, currently-trading SaaS business (facts pulled from pagepilot.ai on 2026-09-28; verify against the live site and the owner's own account before every run, this file is not kept in sync automatically).

## What it is
An AI tool that turns a product URL (from AliExpress, Amazon, Shopify or Etsy) into a Shopify product page in under 60 seconds: a drag-and-drop editor, AI-generated product images, ad creatives and copy, a customisable cart drawer with upsells, works with any Shopify theme, 30+ languages. Built for e-commerce merchants and dropshippers who want a page fast without design or coding skill. Plans: Lite $39/mo (10 pages), Starter $59/mo (unlimited pages, marked "most popular"), Scaler $99/mo (unlimited everything); free trial, no card required.

## Allowed claims
Only what the brand's own site states:
- Builds a Shopify product page from a product link in under 60 seconds.
- No coding or design skill needed; drag-and-drop editor.
- AI-generated product images, ad creatives and copy included.
- Works with any Shopify theme; 30+ languages supported.
- Free trial, no credit card required.
- The site states its merchants have generated over $200M in revenue using it — this is the **brand's own marketing figure**, not independently verified by this project. Use it only if the owner confirms it is still current, and always attribute it to merchants' results, never to a guarantee for a new user.

## Never claim (this project's rule, on top of the brand's own compliance)
- **No income or results guarantees.** The audience is e-commerce merchants and dropshippers, a category the FTC/ASA specifically watches for deceptive earnings claims. Never say a user *will* make money, hit a revenue number, or "quit their job." Speed and ease claims are fine (that is what the tool does); money claims are not ours to make.
- **No "no work required" or "overnight" framing.** PagePilot builds a page; it does not run the business.
- **No claim that it replaces Shopify** — it is a page builder that works inside Shopify.
- **No invented reviews, testimonials or before/after revenue stories.**

## Bridge angles (soft sell)
Bridge on speed and low effort — "build/ship something fast without the usual hassle" — never on money made:
- A trend about scrappy, fast, DIY hacks (get-it-done energy) → "one of mine: I stopped building my Shopify pages by hand — PagePilot turns a product link into a page in under a minute."
- A trend about skipping the boring/slow way of doing something → the same shape: PagePilot skips the design-and-code step.

## Voice
Direct, upbeat, practical — a founder or operator's voice, not a hype-ad voice. Short sentences, sparing emoji.
