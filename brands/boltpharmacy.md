# Bolt Pharmacy · brand profile

Real, currently-trading UK business (facts pulled from boltpharmacy.co.uk on 2026-09-28; verify against the live site and the owner's own account before every run, this file is not kept in sync automatically).

## What it is
A GPhC-registered, CQC-regulated UK online pharmacy (GPhC #9013085) offering clinician-led weight-loss care: a short online eligibility check, a UK clinician reviews the case, then a prescription is issued and delivered (Royal Mail, 1–2 business days after approval). No in-person appointment. Ongoing support and repeat prescriptions through their app; the site says the team replies to messages within about 4 hours.

## Allowed claims
Only service facts stated on the brand's own site, never a medicine name or an outcome:
- GPhC-registered pharmacy, UK-licensed clinicians review every case.
- Online eligibility check, no in-person appointment needed.
- Fast, discreet delivery (1–2 business days after approval).
- Message the team and get a reply within about 4 hours.
- Repeat prescriptions handled through the app.

## Never claim (this project's rule, on top of the brand's own compliance)
- **No medicine names.** Prescription-only medicines cannot be advertised to the public in the UK (Human Medicines Regulations 2012; ASA/CAP code). Talk about the *service* (a clinician review, a prescription sorted online), never the drug.
- **No weight-loss outcome or before/after claims.** No numbers, no timelines, no "you'll lose X," no body imagery. This matches the persona's own do-not rule (`personas/willow.md`: "no body before/afters").
- **No medical advice, no diagnosis, no claim of suitability for the viewer.** The brand's own eligibility check decides that, not the caption.
- **No invented reviews, studies or "doctors recommend."**

## Bridge angles (soft sell)
Bridge on the *service* (fast, low-hassle, handled from your phone), never on weight loss itself, so the connection works for a run whose topic has nothing to do with weight:
- A trend about a busy day or too much to manage → "Bolt Pharmacy works the same way: a quick check online, a UK clinician reviews it, and it's sorted without you having to go anywhere."
- A trend about being told what to do → the service replaces a waiting-room appointment with a short form and a clinician's message.

## Voice
Plain, reassuring, no hype, no urgency language ("don't wait," "limited spots"). Sentence case.
