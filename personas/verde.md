# Verde · illustrated mascot

An original character, made for Trend rider runs that need a mascot-led card format (P005 to P009). It is deliberately unlike @bluebro.fit's blue mascot (different color, head, face treatment and outfit), so a run keeps the source's *format* without copying its *character* (guideline 6, originality). Everything else (topic, poses, props, wording) comes from the pillar and the run.

## Character
- **Head:** smooth rounded helmet-like head, no face. One horizontal glowing amber visor band (`#FFB020`) across where the eyes would be. Nothing else on the face.
- **Body:** solid teal-green (`#16B7A1`) with slightly darker teal cel-shading. Broad shoulders, thick neck and a very developed trapezius, defined chest, abs and arms, thick legs.
- **Wears:** charcoal-grey athletic shorts with a thin white side stripe, plain white training shoes with grey soles, amber fabric wrist wraps on both wrists.
- **Style:** bold western comic-book illustration, cel-shaded, thick dark navy outlines, flat fills with hard-edged shading, visible vein and muscle-separation lines. Gym equipment is drawn in the same style in dark charcoal and light steel grey.
- **Reference:** `personas/verde/refs/sheet.png` (front, side and back views). Attach it, and any earlier art that shows a pose to keep, to every image.
- **Highlight colors for anatomy art (contrast with teal):** red `#E5322D` for the target muscle, yellow `#FFD23F` for a second region. Purple was tried for a third region and dropped (the model fills the wrong muscles).

## Card look (theme in `tools/cards.py`)
- Background chalk `#F2F3EE`, headings charcoal navy `#16202B` in DIN Condensed Bold, accent teal `#0B8A77` (numbers, underline, the last cover line), body gray `#4A5561` in Avenir Next.
- Placeholder brand mark top-left: a teal circle with an amber visor slit plus the wordmark `VERDE FIT`. **Replace the wordmark with the real handle** before posting.

## Use so far
- P005 run-01 (upper traps, 2026-09-25, Trend rider): rated a success by the owner. The model sheet's generation prompt and cost are in `personas/verde/costs.json`; the 10 slide images all used the sheet as a reference.

## Always
- Text is drawn in code (`tools/cards.py`), never by the image model. Prompts say "no text, no letters, no logos".
- TikTok's AI-generated content label on when posted (the art is AI-generated).
- The character is fictional and original: never a real person, never another account's mascot or a franchise character.
