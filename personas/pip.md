# Pip · PagePilot mascot

Original character made for @pagepilot.ai when the owner asked to copy @bluebro.fit's account system (2026-10-06). It plays the role Blue Bro plays on that account (a recurring faceless mascot that acts every tip out) but shares nothing with him: a chunky little pilot, not a muscular figure, with goggles instead of almond eyes, a purple taken from PagePilot's own brand colour, and a pilot theme taken from PagePilot's rocket logo.

## Character
- Big round head and short rounded body, about 3 heads tall, not muscular. Solid purple skin `#4922FF` with darker purple cel-shading, four-fingered hands.
- Faceless: no mouth or nose. Round aviator goggles with glossy pale lavender lenses and dark rims cover the eyes.
- Cream leather aviator cap with ear flaps and a chin strap, short cream-white scarf, dark navy jumpsuit vest with chest pockets and a zip, dark navy rounded boots.
- Style: clean bold 2D cartoon, thick dark navy outlines, flat fills, hard-edged cel shading. Props in the same style in charcoal, grey and cream.
- Reference: `personas/pip/refs/sheet.png` (front, side, back; GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst 2K, 10 credits). Attach it to every image.

Background `#f5f3fb` (lavender-white), headings `#1f1555` (PagePilot dark) in DIN Condensed Bold, accent `#4922ff` (PagePilot purple: numbers, underline, the middle cover line), body `#4b4860` in Avenir Next.
