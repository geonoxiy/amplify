# Willow · persona profile

The basis for every render that includes Willow. She is fictional. The pillar decides the format, scene type, pose, outfit type and hook; this file decides how she looks, moves and reacts, and the places she lives in. Each shot also gets a moment spec (guideline Part 7). Sides are always given from her side and the picture's side.

## 0. Lines to paste into every prompt

* **Identity (locked):** the same woman as the reference photos: Willow, 31, light-olive skin, warm brown eyes, freckles across the nose bridge and upper cheeks, two small flat moles on her left cheek (right side of the picture) and one near her left jaw, oval face with a slightly square jaw, straight nose with a small bump on the bridge, fuller lower lip, dark brown shoulder-length loose waves with frizz and flyaways at the crown, small gold hoops in both ears, natural slightly uneven teeth.
* **Realism:** unretouched iPhone photo, natural skin texture with visible pores and fine facial hair, slight sensor noise, true-to-life colour, no beauty filter, no retouching, no studio lighting.
* **Guards (from 16.7):** her moles are small, flat and subtle, light brown, exactly as in the reference face; no other moles or dark spots on her face. Plain phone case, no logos anywhere.
* **Attach:** `refs/real_face.png` and `refs/sheet_real.png` always; then the body, room, light, state and close-up refs that fit the shot (order and rules in section 16).

## 1. Who she is (only what drives her face, body and home)

* 31, evidence-first wellness educator: explains sleep, movement, food and stress without hype. Dry British humour, deadpan delivery, quick to laugh at herself. Calm, a bit sceptical, warm once she's laughing.
* Fit because she runs three times a week and lifts twice, not because it's her job. Not a fitness model.
* Lives in Bristol, UK, in a rented two-bed flat she shares with a flatmate (section 15). Works from home most days. Walks everywhere, no car.

## 2. Face (locked)

* **Shape:** oval with a slightly square jaw; softly defined cheekbones; medium-high forehead; rounded hairline with baby hairs at the temples.
* **Asymmetry:** her smile lifts more on her left (right of the picture); her left brow sits a touch higher; her left eye is very slightly narrower.
* **Eyes:** warm medium brown with a darker outer ring and lighter amber flecks near the pupil; almond, slightly hooded, visible crease. Medium straight lashes, natural. Light shadows and faint fine lines under the eyes; whites a little pink at the inner corners. In direct sun the iris goes honey-amber.
* **Brows:** medium-full, soft angle, darker brown than her hair at the roots, natural with stray hairs, tails slightly sparse. Brushed, never laminated.
* **Nose:** straight with a small bump on the bridge, medium width, softly rounded tip; slight redness around the nostrils; visible pores on the nose.
* **Lips:** fuller lower lip, soft Cupid's bow, natural rosy-beige, a little dry; at rest closed, corners neutral.
* **Teeth:** her own, slightly uneven: lower front teeth a little crowded, one upper canine slightly forward, natural off-white (see `sheet_real.png`). Never veneers.
* **Ears:** small, close to the head; a gold hoop in each lobe and a second, empty hole in her left lobe. Hair usually tucked behind her left ear, so the left hoop shows.
* **Skin:** light olive, Fitzpatrick III, neutral-olive undertone. Pores on the nose and inner cheeks; fine peach fuzz that shows in side light. Freckles clustered across the nose bridge and upper cheeks, a few on the forehead. Moles: two small flat ones on her left cheek (one at nose height, one lower towards the jaw) and one near her left jaw. Slight redness on the nose and chin. Shine on the forehead, nose and chin by the afternoon. Faint smile lines, crow's feet when she laughs, forehead lines when her brows lift. The odd small spot on her chin or jaw.
* **Scar:** a small pale 1 cm scar under her chin (childhood), only visible from below.
* **Makeup default:** none, or tinted moisturiser, brow gel and lip balm. Mascara and a little concealer for going out. Skin texture always shows.
* **No glasses, no tattoos.**

## 3. Hair

* **Colour:** natural dark brown, never dyed; shows warm chestnut and auburn in sun; two or three grey strands near the crown.
* **Length:** about 2 cm past her shoulders at the back, shorter face-framing pieces at the jaw.
* **Texture:** loose 2B waves, medium-thick, frizz and flyaways at the crown, baby hairs at the hairline, uneven ends.
* **Parting:** soft and loose, slightly off-centre, often pushed back with her fingers rather than combed.
* **Styles:** down (default); tortoiseshell claw clip, half-up or a full twist (at home, cooking); low messy bun or ponytail with a black hair tie (running, gym). Strands always work loose from clips and buns.
* **Habits:** tucks it behind her left ear; pushes it back off her face with her whole hand; it sticks to her neck when she's hot.
* **Wash cycle:** washes every three days. Day 1 bigger, fluffier waves; day 3 flatter roots, more frizz, more clipped up.
* **Wet:** almost black, clumped into strands, waves tighten, sticks to her neck and cheeks.

## 8. Gaze

* Looks into the lens in at most 1 frame in 3, except talking-head and still-stare formats.
* In selfies she looks at the screen, so her eyes land slightly below the lens.
* Looks up and to her right when thinking; looks away mid-sentence and back to land a point; glances at the doorway when the flatmate talks.
* Video: irregular blinks, slower when deadpan.

## 9. Body language

* **Standing:** weight on her right leg, left knee soft, hip out a little. Leans her hip on counters and her shoulder on door frames.
* **Listening or sceptical:** arms crossed, weight back.
* **Hands:** tucks her hair behind her left ear (most frequent); pushes her sleeves up to mid-forearm, unevenly; holds mugs with both hands; fiddles with the hair tie on her wrist; rubs the back of her neck when awkward; a hand to her collarbone when laughing.
* **Sitting:** cross-legged on the sofa or bed; one knee up with her foot on the desk chair; slumped sideways on the sofa in the evening.
* **Phone:** right hand, little finger under the phone, held slightly above eye level for selfies, her arm visible at the edge of the frame.
* **Energy:** calm and economical; gestures small and close to the body; big only when she laughs.

## 10. Movement (video)

* **Walk:** brisk, long stride, light bounce, moderate arm swing (more on the left, because the tote is on her right shoulder), head steady, turns head first.
* **Tempo:** unhurried when talking, quick when walking.
* **Idle:** shifts weight between her legs, touches her hair, adjusts the tote strap, plays with the hair tie.
* **Talking:** small nods on stressed words, the brows do most of the work, one-hand gestures (right), looks away to think.
* **Running:** light and upright, short quick steps, ponytail swinging.
* **Motion source:** body movement comes from motion transfer of real footage (guideline 3.5), never from a text prompt alone.

## 11. Voice

* Southern English with a light West Country edge; low-medium pitch; measured pace that speeds up when she jokes; dry and warm.
* Habits: "right, so", "honestly", a short pause before a punchline, laughs through her nose.

## 12. States

* **Morning:** puffy lids, a pillow crease on her cheek, flat hair on one side, no makeup, slow movements, sleep tee.
* **Post-run:** flushed cheeks, neck and chest, sweat at the hairline and upper lip, damp baby hairs, loose ponytail, darker sweat patch on the chest of her running tee, veins on her forearms.
* **Tired:** darker under-eyes, paler lips, heavier lids, sinking posture.
* **After a shower:** wet hair combed back with her fingers, pink skin, in her sleep tee or the charcoal hoodie.
* **Going out:** mascara, a little concealer, white shirt or rust jumper; skin still textured.
* **Cold:** red nose tip, cheeks and knuckles, sleeves over her hands, shoulders up, puffer zipped to the chin.
* **Hot:** sheen on her forehead and upper lip, hair clipped up, sleeves pushed up, fanning herself.

### 16.7 Generation notes (from making this set)

* **Moles blow up.** In about 1 take in 5 the model turned her small moles into a cluster of big dark spots. Add: "Her moles are small, flat and subtle, light brown, exactly as in the reference face; no other moles or dark spots on her face." Reject any take where they've grown.
* **"Selfie" draws the phone.** A front-camera selfie prompt drew a visible phone and hand (twice). Write: "The photo is the selfie itself, taken with the front camera; the phone and her hand holding it are not in the picture" (guideline 2.17 terms).
* **Logos creep in.** One take showed an Apple logo on the phone case, one a skincare brand, one a car badge. Ask for "plain case with no logo" and check every take.
* **Edits polish.** Fixing a take with a Nano Banana Pro edit made her hair longer, smoother and glossier; a re-roll with the fix in the prompt was better.
* **Identity drift.** About 1 take in 4 drifted to a different face (rounder, younger, or a generic influencer face), most often in full-body and three-quarter shots. Two takes per shot was enough to get one right for every shot but two.
* **Hoops render chunky** in close-ups; say "small thin gold hoops" if it matters.
