## 6. Language style
- English, second person, imperative ("Choose one of each", "Eat in a Healthy Deficit"); tips carry numbers and ranges (`0.7-1g per lb`, `400-800 calorie deficit`, `7-8 hours`, `2-3 Sets x 6-10 Reps`). Some authority phrasing ("Studies have shown…").
- Captions: one short line that restates the promise, 3 to 5 hashtags, no emoji (except the one funnel caption).
- **Typos are frequent** (8 posts): `HUMANY`, `pefect`, `percect`, `positon`, `srarts`, `desinated`, `pgroram`, `exericse`, `uss`; the slide order glitch (`#5` before `#4`), a repeated leg day. They read as unproofed, not as style; do not replicate them.
- Hashtags: 3 to 5 per post, topic-specific and niche (`#hypertrophy`, `#gymtips`, `#pullday`), no broad tags, one product tag (`#healthmeter`); top used: `#gymtips` 7, `#gymworkout` 6, `#healthmeter` 5, `#hypertrophy` 5.
- Sounds: all 20 use a sound made by someone else (4 are another creator's "original sound"); slideshows use background music only; the one voiceover is the shoulder reel (short 2 s lines: "Front delt. Side delt. Rear delt. Follow for more.").
