## 9. Hook formula(s)
- `[confession]. please give me your most unhinged [category] hacks. i'm not talking [obvious safe example] on the side - i wanna hear something crazy but you don't regret it`
- Variants: `[confession]. what's the most unhinged thing you've done for [goal]? not [safe example], i want the real stuff` · `give me your most feral [category] tips. i'll wait`

**Mechanics:** a confession lowers the reader's guard (relatable), the request for "unhinged" answers is a dare that people want to answer to seem interesting, and ruling out the obvious example first raises the bar so replies escalate. The face sells the desperation before a word is read; comments become the payoff instead of a reveal slide.
**Reference example (evidence only, don't reuse):** "I'm incredibly broke, please give me your most unhinged money making hacks. I'm not talking dooordash on the side - i wanna hear something crazy but you don't regret it"

## 10. Slide text rules
- One block, 35 to 45 words, 6 to 8 short centred lines.
- Sentence case, first person, a lowercase dash before the final clause, no full stop at the end. Self-deprecating, escalating tone.
- No emoji on the slide.

## 14. Do / Don't
- Do: keep it to one slide; put the whole hook in one text block; rule out an obvious answer before asking for something wild; keep the face exaggerated and comedic.
- Don't: add a second slide, a product shot or a CTA on the image.
- **Don't (AI replication):** never recreate the source's face, and never render a real person's likeness. Use a fictional persona.
- **Originality:** the confession, category and ruled-out example must all be new; keep the shared words with the source under 30%.
- **AI production note:** one still image, persona reference + text drawn in code. Ask for an exaggerated, comedic expression, not a sincere or distressing one (see P012's Do/Don't on invented outcomes and sincerity — this pillar is the comedic register, not P012's earnest one).
