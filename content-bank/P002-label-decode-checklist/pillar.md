## 2. Summary
A protective "how to actually check" checklist that turns a common buying worry into numbered checks, a do-it-yourself verification step, a list of banned items, and red flags to walk away from.

## 3. Best for
- Categories where buyers worry about fakes, safety or scams: skincare, supplements, gadgets, online shops, rentals, financial apps.
- Positioning a brand as the trustworthy option: the brand passes every check.
- Not for: entertainment, emotional stories, or products that would fail their own checklist.

## 9. Hook formula(s)
- `[LABEL]` / `a new [product type] drops on your feed every single day.` / `safe?` / `here's how to actually check 👇`
- Variants: `[product type] everywhere on your fyp.` / `legit?` · `everyone's selling [product type] now.` / `real?`

**Mechanics:** it names a feeling everyone has scrolling TikTok Shop ("too many new brands") and shrinks it into one huge word (`safe?`). That's a safety stake plus a promise of a method, so people swipe to protect themselves and save it for later.

**Reference example (evidence only):** "a new skincare brand drops on your feed every single day. / safe? / here's how to actually check 👇"

## 10. Slide text rules
- 15–35 words per slide: a title plus list cards, each card a **bold item** and one short detail line.
- Lowercase body; UPPERCASE pill labels; "NEVER" in caps is the one allowed emphasis.
- Second person, protective big-sister tone.
- Numbered checks (`CHECK #1-2`) so viewers feel progress.
- Emoji only as markers: 👇 🚩 ⚠️ 🚫 🔍.
