## 2. Summary
A ranking carousel that counts items down from worst to best, each with a colored x/10 score badge, a one-line verdict and a picture, from a mascot-led cover.

## 3. Best for
- Opinion-led comparisons that invite disagreement: workout splits, exercises, tools, routines, products (ranked and scored).
- Accounts that want comments and shares without selling.
- Not for: step-by-step teaching, or topics with fewer than 5 comparable items.

## 4. Structure
6 to 8 slides: hook → 5 to 7 ranked items counting down (worst first) → no CTA.

| # | Role | What happens | Text on screen (pattern, not the source's words) |
|---|------|--------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Hook | Ranking headline in italic condensed type over the mascot; either surrounded by 4 calendar cards naming the items, or working out on the equipment with the target muscle red. An optional pill tagline under the title. | `RANKING [THINGS]` or `TOP [N] [THINGS]` + pill `[ACTUALLY WORTH DOING]` |
| 2..n | Ranked item | `#N [Item name]`, underline, a rounded score badge (`x/10`), a one-line verdict, then a picture: a weekly split strip of mascots with the trained muscles in red (a red X over skipped days), or the mascot on the exercise's equipment on a grey circle. | `#N [Name]` · `[score]/10` · `[one sentence verdict]` |

The badge color runs red (1/10) → orange (3) → yellow (6) → green (7) → dark green (9 to 11): the color is the score.

## 5. Product slot
None. The format sells nothing; it earns comments (disagreement) and shares.

## 6. Proof device
The score badge and the picture are the proof: the split strip (a diagram of the week, muscles in red) or the exercise drawing. There are no screenshots.

## 7. Series structure
Single post.

Not applicable (slideshow).

## 9. Hook formula(s)
- `RANKING [ALL/POPULAR] [THINGS]` over a mascot with 4 named calendar cards.
- `TOP [N] [THINGS]` + pill tagline `ACTUALLY WORTH DOING`.
Mechanics: a ranking is a verdict, so the viewer swipes to see where their own choice lands; the countdown saves the best for last; a joke score at the end (`11/10`) rewards finishing.
Reference examples (evidence only): "RANKING GYM SPLITS", "TOP 5 CHEST EXERCISES / ACTUALLY WORTH DOING".

## 10. Slide text rules
- Item title: Title Case, humanist bold sans (not the condensed heading font); verdict: one sentence of 8 to 20 words, opinionated, may use slang or `IMO`.
- Scores: `x/10`, decimals allowed (`9.5/10`); the best item may exceed 10 as a joke.
- Numbering must count down and stay in order (one source post shows #5 before #4: do not copy).

## 12. Caption formula and hashtag pattern
- Caption: a superlative or verdict line (`Ranking all popular … from worst to best for …!`, `GOATED … actually worth doing!`) + 3 hashtags. No emoji, no CTA.
- Hashtags: 3, topic-specific (`#gymsplits #workoutroutine #musclegrowthtips`).

## 14. Do / Don't
- Do: keep the brand system fixed: beige card, mascot, logo top-left, the underline under every heading, the same two fonts.
- Do: draw every prop the tip mentions (the mascot holds the food, sits on the machine, wears the outfit).
- Do: leave the top-right of content slides empty; the eye goes heading → text → illustration.
- Don't: use photos, faces or a second character. The account never shows a real person.
- Don't: copy the source's typos (`positon`, `HUMANY`, `pefect`, `srarts`); they are errors, not style.
- Don't: reuse the source's exact wording, exercises order or drawings (originality check).
- Do: color the badge by the score, and count down in order.
- Don't: add a product slide; the format works because it sells nothing.
