## 3. Best for

- Not for: products with no real public discussion. This format must never be faked (see Don't).

## 9. Hook formula(s)
- Slide 1 is the real question post. Pick threads whose title already works as a hook: `musta ang [product] experience mo?` / `is [product] worth the hype?` / `anyone tried [product] for [use]?`
- Caption hook: `effective ba talaga si [product]??? share your experience sa comments!`

**Mechanics:** a real person's doubt mirrors the viewer's own doubt, so it's instant identification. Screenshots signal "unfiltered, not an ad". Comment slides pull viewers through a mini-debate, and the caption asks them to add their own experience (comment bait).

## 10. Slide text rules
No text is added on the slides. All words come from the thread. Selection rules: 1 question + 5 replies; mix lengths (one-liner, long, medium); Taglish is fine and adds local authenticity; at least one caution for balance.

## 11. Visual style
**Visual class:** web or app UI screenshots (Reddit forum thread, dark mode). All text is in-image UI text, not overlay text. Nothing is placed by us except the crop.

- Canvas: tall screenshots, one per slide, cropped tight to the post/comment (no border, no designed background).
- Reddit dark mode (near-black background, light grey text); subreddit badge and "Join" button visible on slide 1; vote counts and reply icons visible.
- Every username and avatar blurred or covered.
- Nested replies shown as the thread's own indentation (slide 5).

## 12. Caption formula and hashtag pattern
- **Formula:** Taglish question to the viewer about the product (`effective ba talaga si [product]???`) + comment prompt (`share your experience sa comments!`) + hashtags. Short: one line.

## 14. Do / Don't
- **Do:** use only real, public threads; blur every username and avatar; keep a balanced mix (joke, success, score, problem, caution); credit the community on slide 1 (badge visible); check the thread's claims aren't dangerous before posting.
- **Don't:** write, edit or invent reviews, comments or scores. Fabricated reviews of a real product are deceptive and break ad rules. Don't crop out cautions to make it one-sided, and don't show personal info.
- **Replication rule (for Amplify):** the agent reproduces this format by finding a real public thread about the brand's product and selecting and ordering screenshots per this pillar. It does not render fake threads. If no real thread exists, use a different pillar.
