## 2. Summary
A motivation-hook carousel that shows a mascot body transformation, a one-day meal plan and a 6-day training week as exercise-card grids (with a rest day), sold through the caption or a bonus-tip slide.

## 3. Best for
- Aspirational "everything you need" posts: a full program (what to eat, what to do each day) in one save-worthy carousel.
- Funnel posts that push viewers to a product (comment-to-DM) or an app.
- Not for: tips or opinions; it is a reference sheet.

## 4. Structure
8 to 9 slides: hook → meal plan → 6 day cards (a rest day is one of them in 1 of 2 posts) → [bonus tip].

| # | Role | What happens | Text on screen (pattern, not the source's words) |
|---|------|--------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Hook | A one-word or short motivational title over the mascot's transformation (2 stages with a `>>` arrow, or 3 stages labelled `1 MONTH / 6 MONTHS / 12 MONTHS`). | `THE POWER OF [VIRTUE]` / `[VIRTUE]` (one word, blue) |
| 2 | Meal plan | Three cards (breakfast, lunch, dinner): mascot torso icon at the left, bullet list of foods, a plate illustration at the right, calories and protein in bold under each; optional header. | `BREAKFAST / LUNCH / DINNER` · `[n] kcal, [n]g Protein` · optional `DAILY TOTAL: [n] kcal, [n]g Protein` |
| 3..8 | Program day | `#N [Day name]` heading and underline, then a 3×2 grid of exercise cards: dark numbered circle, exercise name in grey condensed type, the mascot on the machine, `2-3 Sets x [6-8 or 8-12] Reps`. In 1 of 2 posts one day is a rest day: a single mascot in a hoodie on an armchair with a remote. | `#N [Upper Body / Lower Body / Push Day / Pull Day / Leg Day + Abs / Rest Day]` |
| 9 | Bonus tip (optional) | `Bonus Tip` with a phone mockup held by a mascot hand. | `Bonus Tip` + `Track what you actually eat, not what you think you eat. I use [app].` |

## 5. Product slot
Two variants: (1) no product slide; the caption is a hard-sell funnel: a benefit list and `Comment "[KEYWORD]" and I'll DM you the link` to the eBook (post 1); (2) the last slide is the app plug bonus tip and the caption tags the app (post 2). Both are in addition to the bio link.

## 6. Proof device
The meal-plan cards (calories and protein) and the exercise-card grids act as the "plan". The app phone mockup is a UI screenshot.

## 7. Series structure
Single post (the whole week in one). Not split across posts.

## 9. Hook formula(s)
- `THE POWER OF [VIRTUE]` (navy + blue) over a lean-to-muscular pair.
- `[ONE-WORD VIRTUE]` over a 3-stage timeline (`1 MONTH / 6 MONTHS / 12 MONTHS`).
Mechanics: identity and aspiration (the picture shows who you become), a promise of a complete plan, and the structure (food + a week of training) makes people save it. This is the account's best-performing format by reach.
Reference examples (evidence only): "THE POWER OF DISCIPLINE", "CONSISTENCY".

## 10. Slide text rules
- Titles in ALL CAPS on the cover; day headings in Title Case with `#N`.
- Cards contain only names and `2-3 Sets x N-N Reps`; no paragraphs.
- Meal cards: foods with weights (`200g chicken breast`), macros in bold.
- The caption may be a long sales pitch (the only post with emoji and a bullet list).

**Visual class:** Other, `designed-card` layout, with `2d-characters` (the mascot) in the cards; one slide (rest day) is a single mascot illustration.
- **Canvas:** 4:5 portrait, 1080×1350 (one source post is 1080×1338). No bars, no border. Code-measured over 106 slides: brightness 70–90%, saturation median 10.9%, sharpness median 673, clutter median 4.0%, no letterbox bars.
- **Background:** flat warm beige `#f4f1ea` on every slide. No gradient, texture or photo.
- **Colors (sampled in code):** accent blue `#0375d8` (rank numbers, the underline, the highlighted cover line), navy `#051b3a` (headings), body gray `#4b4946`, mascot blue `#0270f9`, black `#151618` (shorts, equipment), red `#e2002a` (muscle highlights, score-badge red), card grey `#ebe7df`. The account-wide clusters are in `stats.json` (`brand_palette`).
- **Logo:** BLUE BRO wordmark (black condensed caps with the blue mascot-head icon at the left), top-left, box `[2.7, 2.1, 14.4, 4.2]` on every slide. A fixed watermark.
- **Fonts (closest match):** headings Anton-like condensed heavy grotesque; body Didact Gothic-like light humanist sans; ranking slides use a humanist bold sans (Open Sans Bold-like) for the item title.

Equipment and props are drawn realistically in black and grey. The same design on every slide, so recreating it needs a model sheet.
- **Cover template (text placement: top-headline):** 2 to 3 stacked lines, centered, x 4–96%, y 14.8–44%, cap height 6–9% of the frame height; the first line is navy, the last line accent blue; the mascot fills the lower 55% (y 44–100%) and bleeds off the bottom edge.
- **Content template (text placement: numbered-heading):** rank number `#N` at `[10, 16.6, 8.3, 6.6]` (accent blue, slightly italic); title at `[24.4, 17.8, up to 60 wide, 5.0]` (navy, Title Case); a short blue underline at `[10, 26.6, 12.6, 0.5]`; body at `[10, 31.3, up to 65 wide, up to 20 tall]` (gray, about 2.7% font size, left-aligned, 2 to 4 lines); the illustration in the lower half.
- **Exercise-card grid:** a 3×2 grid at `[2.7, 35.2, 94.5, 57.4]`; each card about 30% wide and 28% tall, light grey `#ebe7df`, rounded; number badge (dark circle, white numeral) and name at the top-left, the mascot on a black machine in the middle, `2-3 Sets x 6-8 Reps` centered at the bottom.
- **Meal card:** three stacked cards; the mascot torso icon at the left, bullet list in the middle (body font), a realistic plate at the right, `kcal` and `g Protein` in bold condensed type.
- **Fixed:** the grid layout and card style, the underline heading, the logo. **Varies:** which days, exercise lists, the transformation art, the last slide.

## 12. Caption formula and hashtag pattern
- Variant 1 (funnel): motivation line → 5 bullets of what the product gives (`Structured workouts`, `Built-in recovery`, …) → `Comment "[KEYWORD]" and I'll DM you the link.` → hashtags. Uses emoji.
- Variant 2 (plug): theme line + `Using @[app] to keep track of …` + hashtags including `#[app]`.
- Hashtags: 5, mixed: broad or CamelCase (`#MuscleGrowth #Hypertrophy #GymWorkout`) in the funnel post; topic plus product tag in the plug post.

## 14. Do / Don't
- Do: keep the brand system fixed: beige card, mascot, logo top-left, the underline under every heading, the same two fonts.
- Do: draw every prop the tip mentions (the mascot holds the food, sits on the machine, wears the outfit).
- Do: leave the top-right of content slides empty; the eye goes heading → text → illustration.
- Don't: use photos, faces or a second character. The account never shows a real person.
- Don't: copy the source's typos (`positon`, `HUMANY`, `pefect`, `srarts`); they are errors, not style.
- Don't: reuse the source's exact wording, exercises order or drawings (originality check).
- Do: include a meal plan; a rest day (1 of 2 source posts) also makes the plan feel complete.
- Do: keep the grid identical across days so only names and drawings change.
- Don't: repeat the same day twice (one source post repeats its leg-day grid on slides 5 and 8).
