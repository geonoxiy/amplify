# P016 learnings

Feedback from runs, turned into general rules.

## Boss's test, before this pillar existed (2026-10-05)

A first Bolt Pharmacy attempt from the same Snidin screenshot (made on the boss's machine, persona "Helen") kept the
white text boxes but put the presenter in her kitchen and wrote flat, leaflet-style lists ("a few things worth knowing
about mounjaro"). The boss's verdict: it copied the text format only; the visual hook (the medical setting that makes
people see authority) and the viral hooks were lost. A second request ("make it actually informational and viral")
still didn't get there. A customer will use the tool directly, so the first output has to be right.

Rules taken from it (also in `.claude/skills/amplify-plus/SKILL.md`):
- The professional scene is the format. Never move the post to a persona's home; put the worker in the brand's own
  kind of workplace.
- Write hooks with the source's mechanics and register (see pillar section 9), not a safe summary.
- Hide the product as one tip mid-list, in the same voice; make the rest of the list genuinely useful.
- For Bolt: never name Mounjaro (prescription-only medicine; UK ban on advertising them to the public). The product
  tip is the service. No qualification claimed for an AI worker.
