# P001 – Ingredient spotlight

- https://www.tiktok.com/@smartshopper858/photo/7684431864446127380 (glycolic acid, 7 slides)

## 2. Summary
A calm, designed-card explainer that takes one ingredient (or feature) people see everywhere and turns it into a save-worthy cheat sheet: what it is, why it works, how much is safe, how long results take, and what to pair it with.

## 3. Best for
- Any product whose value depends on one "hero" ingredient, material, feature or setting: skincare actives, supplements, food ingredients, fabrics, app features, finance terms.
- Building trust before selling: it reads as research, not an ad.
- Not for: emotional stories, launches, or anything that needs a face or a demo.

## 4. Structure
7–8 slides, one idea per slide, always in this order (slide 3 is optional):

| # | Role | What happens | Text on screen (pattern) |
|---|------|--------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Hook | Name the thing big, tell the viewer to check what they own | `INGREDIENT SPOTLIGHT` / **[thing]** / `check your [category]! 👀` / `what is it, really?` |
| 2 | Definition | One-line plain definition + where it's commonly found | `what is it?` / `[plain one-line definition]` / `COMMONLY FOUND IN` + 3 chips |
| 3 | Comparison *(optional)* | Clear up a common mix-up with a look-alike | `KNOW THE DIFFERENCE` / 2 cards: **[thing]** (short trait) vs. **[look-alike]** (short trait, faded) / `this post is about [thing] only 👇` |
| 4 | Mechanism | Why it works as 3 steps with arrows | `why it works` / `[benefit 1] → [benefit 2] → [benefit 3]` (2–3 words each) |
| 5 | Dosage | A "safe %" or safe-amount scale, beginner to max | `SAFE % CHART` / slider with 3 marks: `[low] beginners` · `[mid] moderate/typical max` · `[high] typical max/pro only` |
| 6 | Timeline | Honest expectations as 3 stages | `HONEST TIMELINE` / `[week range] [how often]` → `[week range] [build up]` → `[time] [real result]` |
| 7 | Pairing | What goes well with it vs. what to avoid | `✅ PAIR WITH` 2 items / `🚫 AVOID SAME [time]` 2 items |
| 8 | Disclaimer + CTA | Soft disclaimer, a safety note, and a follow prompt | `not [medical/financial/…] advice, just research` / `NOTE` [who should be careful] / `NEXT` `follow for more [thing] breakdowns 🔍` |

## 5. Product slot
None on the slides. The format sells by trust: no brand is named. If a brand wants its product in, add it at most as a chip on slide 2 (`COMMONLY FOUND IN: … [our product]`) or as the first ✅ item on slide 7. Never make a whole slide about the product; that breaks the "just research" feel.

## 6. Proof device
None. Credibility comes from specifics (percentages, weeks, named pairings), not screenshots.

## 7. Series structure
Recurring series: same template, a different ingredient each post. Posted in bursts (both sources went up within an hour of each other on the same evening). Works as a numbered or named series ("ingredient spotlight: [x]").

## 8. Video timing (videos only)
Not applicable (slideshow).

## 9. Hook formula(s)
- `INGREDIENT SPOTLIGHT` / `[thing]` / `check your [category]! 👀` / `what is it, really?`
- Caption hook: `check ur [category] 👀 [thing] is everywhere, but do u actually know what it does?`
- Caption hook (with a mix-up): `check ur [category] 👀 [thing] is everywhere but it's NOT the same as [look-alike] ([why it matters]).`

**Mechanics:** the giant name makes people stop if they recognize it ("wait, that's in my serum"). "Check your skincare" turns it into a personal task, and "what is it, really?" opens a curiosity gap that the next slide closes. Stakes are low and friendly, so people swipe to learn, not out of fear.

**Reference examples (evidence only, don't reuse):** "retinol / check your skincare! 👀 / what is it, really?" · "glycolic acid / check your skincare! 👀 / what is it, really?"

## 10. Slide text rules
- 5–15 words per slide; one idea per slide.
- All lowercase, except the small pill labels (UPPERCASE, letter-spaced).
- Second person, calm expert-friend tone; no hype words, no exclamation marks except in the hook.
- Numbers wherever possible (%, weeks, times per week).
- Emoji: 👀 in the hook, ✅ 🚫 on the pairing slide, 🔍 at the end. Nowhere else.
- English with PH context allowed (e.g. PH prices, local brands) but slide text stays English.

## 11. Visual style
**Visual class:** Other, `designed-card` (text and simple graphics on a built background, no photo; proposed as a class in `content-bank/_other-classes.md`). Text placement patterns seen: stacked card (the centered hook) and icon lockup (icon left, text stack right, on the definition slide). Box ranges are not recorded yet.

- **Canvas:** 1080×1080 square. No photos: designed cards only.
- **Background:** one flat pastel per slide, rotating in this order: pink `#fde0e5` → blue `#dbedfb` → yellow `#fcf2cf` → green `#e0f2e4` → lilac `#eddff6` → repeat. Two soft, blurred blobs of a slightly darker tint in opposite corners (top right and bottom left).
- **Title text:** bold rounded sans (closest match: Inter Bold or SF Pro Rounded Bold), in a deep shade of the slide's pastel (deep rose on pink, forest green on green, and so on; exact text hex not measured). The slide 1 name is huge (about 10% of canvas height) with a soft glow.
- **Pill labels:** small uppercase letter-spaced text inside a rounded pill tinted a shade darker than the background, centered above the title.
- **Icons:** thin line icons inside white circles with a soft shadow (moon, drop, layers, clock, check).
- **Cards:** white or tinted rounded rectangles with a soft shadow for comparisons and lists.
- **Layout:** everything centered, lots of empty space; content sits in the middle 60% of the canvas.
- **Watermark:** small grey search icon + account name, bottom center, on every slide.

## 12. Caption formula and hashtag pattern
- **Formula:** hook line (repeats slide 1) → what you get if you swipe (`swipe for the safe % to start at, an honest timeline, and what to pair it with`) → soft disclaimer (`not medical advice, just research 🔬`) → who should be careful → `follow for more [thing] breakdowns 🔍` → hashtags.
- **Voice:** casual lowercase, texting spelling (`ur`, `u`), 3–5 emojis spread through the caption. More casual than the slides.
- **Hashtags:** 5, the same fixed set on every post in the series: 2 shopping-culture tags (#Budol, #TikTokMadeMeBuyIt), 2 niche tags (#SkincareTips, #SkincarePH), 1 trend tag (#skinimalism). Swap the niche and trend tags for the brand's category; keep the shopping-culture pair.

## 14. Do / Don't
- **Do:** keep one idea per slide; use real, checkable numbers; keep the disclaimer end card; keep the pastel rotation and the watermark; make slide 5 (the safe amount) and slide 6 (the timeline) the most useful, since they're the reason to save.
- **Don't:** name or push a product on the slides; use photos or faces; use more than 15 words on a slide; use ALL CAPS outside the pill labels; invent dosage numbers (verify them for every new ingredient).
