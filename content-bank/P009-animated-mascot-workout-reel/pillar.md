## 2. Summary
A 16-second reel of an AI-animated mascot doing 6 exercises in a row, with a tracker of 6 muscle icons that ticks one more exercise per cut, a title, and the exercise name with sets and reps; music only.

## 3. Best for
- "Perfect [muscle group] workout" content that must be understood with the sound off and saved as a checklist.
- Any list of N steps or items that can each be shown as one animated illustration (a routine, a recipe, a setup).

## 9. Hook formula(s)
- On screen: `The Perfect [Muscle group] Workout` (Title Case, condensed).
- Caption: `The perfect [muscle group] workout in 6 exercises!`
Mechanics: the promise of a complete answer ("perfect", "6 exercises"), and the tracker makes it a countdown the viewer wants to see completed. The first exercise is already moving at 0 s, so there is no dead start.
Reference examples (evidence only): "The Perfect Pull Workout", "The perfect leg day for muscle growth in 6 exercises!"
