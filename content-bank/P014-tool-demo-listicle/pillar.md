## 10. Slide text rules
- Cover: a headline of 4 to 6 words plus one emoji, and a subheading of 3 to 5 words, both Title Case.
- Tool slides: a heading of 2 to 5 words (tool or feature name) plus one emoji, and a one-line subheading (5 to 10 words) that states the single benefit, Title Case with an occasional lowercase aside, one emoji allowed.
- No paragraphs; every line is short enough to read in under a second.

## 14. Do / Don't
- Do: keep the cover's payoff-plus-credibility shape; show something real on every slide (a loaded screen, not a blank mockup); keep every text line short.
- Don't: add a hard CTA slide; let a subheading run past one line.
- **Don't (AI replication):** never recreate a real, identifiable person's face or an existing brand's exact UI/logo pixel-for-pixel. Generate an original desk scene; draw or paste any product name/logo shown on a screen in code (image models garble small text and logos).
- **Originality:** the tools or features shown, the wording of every heading and subheading, and the desk scene must all be new.
- **AI production note:** the cover needs a persona still (face optional, since the source cover shows no face); the tool-demo slides need a laptop-and-hands still with a plain or gently stylised screen area for a heading/logo to be pasted onto in code, not generated as real text.
