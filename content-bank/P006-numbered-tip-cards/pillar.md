## 2. Summary
A how-to or regret-hook carousel of numbered tip cards, each a heading, a short paragraph and the mascot acting the tip out, ending on an eBook CTA or a soft app-plug bonus tip.

## 3. Best for
- Listicle education where each tip is a rule plus a reason (fat loss, muscle gain, macros, gym tips by muscle).
- Accounts that promote a digital product or an app and want the plug to sit inside useful content (3 of 5 source posts plug an app, 1 sells the eBook).
- Not for: visual comparisons (use P007), long procedures with diagrams (use P005).

## 4. Structure
6 to 9 slides: hook → 5 to 6 numbered tips → [bonus tip, eBook CTA, or nothing].

| # | Role | What happens | Text on screen (pattern, not the source's words) |
|---|------|--------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Hook | Headline over the mascot; often a before/after pair (softer or thinner mascot, `>>` arrow, then the transformed one), or the mascot with a prop (dumbbell, notebook and food). | `HOW TO [OUTCOME] AS FAST AS HUMANLY POSSIBLE` or `I WISH I KNEW THESE [TOPIC] TIPS SOONER (PT. N)` or `HOW TO FIGURE OUT YOUR [X]` |
| 2..n | Tip | `#N [Tip title]`, underline, 1 to 2 short paragraphs (rule, then reason or a number), the mascot at the bottom-right acting the tip out with one prop (steak and eggs, banana, bed, dumbbell, phone). | `#N [Imperative tip title]` + `[rule with a number]. / [why it works].` |
| n+1 | Bonus tip or CTA (optional) | Either a `Bonus Tip` slide with a phone mockup of an app (held by a mascot hand or standing beside), or the eBook CTA (same slide as in P005). | `Bonus Tip` / `Want A Better Physique?` |

Steps variant (macro post): `Step N: [action]` headings, a worked example in the body, a `Recap` slide, then the bonus tip.

## 5. Product slot
Four placements seen: (1) a hard CTA slide for the eBook (1 of 5); (2) a `Bonus Tip` slide as the last item, with a phone mockup (2 of 5: one tells viewers to search the app in the store and shows the app icon; one has the phone in a mascot hand); (3) one inline line inside a tip (`I use [app] to track my macros & calories`) with a phone mockup beside the mascot (1 of 5); (4) no plug at all (1 of 5). The 3 app plugs also tag the app in the caption (`@[app]`, `#[app]`). ## 6. Proof device
Phone mockup of a nutrition app's screen (calories, protein, carbs, fat) placed in the last slide or in the plug tip.

## 7. Series structure
`(PT. 1)` and `(PT. 2)` posts 4 days apart; part 1's caption asks `Who wants to see part 2?` (engagement bait), part 2 continues the numbering with new muscles and ends on the bonus tip.

## 9. Hook formula(s)
- `HOW TO [OUTCOME] AS FAST AS HUMANLY POSSIBLE` over a before/after mascot pair.
- `I WISH I KNEW THESE [TOPIC] TIPS SOONER (PT. N)` (regret plus a series counter).
- `HOW TO FIGURE OUT YOUR [X]` (a how-to for a calculation).
Mechanics: a speed or regret promise (stakes), the before/after image makes the outcome visible, and numbered tips give a clear count to swipe through.
Reference examples (evidence only): "HOW TO CUT BODY FAT AS FAST AS HUMANY (sic) POSSIBLE", "HOW TO BUILD MUSCLE AS FAST AS HUMANLY POSSIBLE", "I WISH I KNEW THESE GYM TIPS SOONER (PT. 1)", "HOW TO FIGURE OUT YOUR MACRO INTAKE".

## 10. Slide text rules
- Tip titles: imperative, Title Case, 2 to 5 words. Body: 15 to 45 words in 1 to 2 short paragraphs; the rule states a number or range (`0.7-1g per lb`, `400-800 calorie deficit`, `7-8 hours`).
- Second person for tips, first person only for the app plug (`I use …`).
- No emoji. Sentence case body. The source has typos and casual spellings (`u`, `i'll`); do not copy them.

- **Tip illustration:** the mascot occupies the lower right, about 44% wide and 41% tall (`[43, 53, 44, 41]`), half-body or full-body, acting the tip out; one realistic prop (food is drawn more realistically than the mascot).
- **Phone mockup slide (estimated from the contact sheets, not box-checked):** a phone UI screenshot at the lower right, roughly a third of the width, tall enough to bleed off the bottom, optionally held by a blue mascot hand.
- **Fixed:** everything in the shared brand block, the tip layout, numbering with `#N`. **Varies:** cover art (before/after pair vs prop), the prop per tip, the closing slide (CTA, bonus tip, none).

## 12. Caption formula and hashtag pattern
- Caption: the promise in one line, then (in 3 of 5) `Using @[app] to track my calories` or `I use @[app] …`, then 3 to 5 hashtags. Part 1 of a series ends with a question.
- Hashtags: 3 to 5: topic (`#musclegrowthtips #gymtips`), a product tag (`#[app]`), sometimes one broad tag (`#gymtok`). The macro post includes a Spanish-language tag (`#macronutrientes`).

## 14. Do / Don't
- Do: keep the brand system fixed: beige card, mascot, logo top-left, the underline under every heading, the same two fonts.
- Do: draw every prop the tip mentions (the mascot holds the food, sits on the machine, wears the outfit).
- Do: leave the top-right of content slides empty; the eye goes heading → text → illustration.
- Don't: use photos, faces or a second character. The account never shows a real person.
- Don't: copy the source's typos (`positon`, `HUMANY`, `pefect`, `srarts`); they are errors, not style.
- Don't: reuse the source's exact wording, exercises order or drawings (originality check).
- Do: put the plug where the reader is already learning (bonus tip, or inside a tip), not as a separate ad slide.
- Do: give every tip a number and a one-line reason.
- Don't: skip the mascot prop; the drawing is what the tip is remembered by.
