# Brand Name · brand profile

Used only when a run's caption style is `hard` or `soft`. A `none` run never loads it, except to scan for leaks.

## What it is
One or two sentences: what the product is, who buys it, what it costs (if public).

## Allowed claims
Only what the owner can back up. Anything not listed here is not claimed.

## Bridge angles (soft sell)
Ways the brand can be tied to a trend, from natural to stretched. The stretch is allowed; a false claim is not.
- e.g. trend about slowing down → "that's the whole idea behind [product]"

## Voice
How the brand writes captions (person, tone, emoji), if it differs from the pillar's caption voice.
