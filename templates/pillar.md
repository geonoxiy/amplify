## 2. Summary

## 3. Best for

## 4. Structure
Slide or shot count (range) and the role of each, in order, e.g. hook → context → tip → tip → product → CTA.

## 5. Product slot
Which slide or shot the product sits on, how it is worked into the story, soft vs. hard sell. "None" if the source never sells.

## 6. Proof device
The phone artifact used as proof, if any (chat thread, screenshot, map, receipt, notes app). "None" otherwise.

## 7. Series structure
For multi-post formats: episode arc, cliffhangers, where the product appears across episodes. "Single post" otherwise.

## 8. Video timing (videos only)
Length, the first 2 seconds, shot list with timings, pacing (cuts per second), camera setup and movement, voiceover or sound, on-screen text timing.

## 9. Hook formula(s)
Fill-in-the-blank templates, e.g. `[ingredient] is everywhere but it's NOT [common confusion]`.
Hook mechanics: why it works (credibility, curiosity gap, stakes) and how slide 1 / the first 2 seconds earn the swipe.
Reference examples: the source hooks, quoted only as evidence.

## 10. Slide text rules
Words per slide, capitalization, tone, point of view, language, punctuation, emoji.

## 11. Visual style
Visual class (main, plus any others in the mix; guideline 2.16). For selfies, the exact term: selfie / person taking a selfie / photo of a selfie / mirror selfie (guideline 2.17). Canvas and aspect, image type, composition, layout, colors (hex), text overlay (font or closest match, size, color, box/outline, position), recurring elements. Give the text placement pattern with its box range in % of the frame (guideline 3.2), and list which visual parts are fixed across the source posts and which vary (guideline 3.6).

## 12. Caption formula and hashtag pattern
Caption structure and voice; hashtag count, types, core set vs. rotating set.

## 13. Performance context
Views, likes, saves and rates of the source posts. For reference only.
